# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard – Excel Project
![Coffee Shop Sale](https://github.com/132006-bhumi/coffee_sales/blob/main/Coffee%20design.jpeg)
# 🔍 Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Excel dashboard analyzing the retail sales performance of a coffee shop. It provides insights into customer behavior, sales trends by time and location, and product preferences. The goal is to extract actionable insights that help improve store operations, enhance product offerings, and optimize sales across multiple dimensions like time, product, and geography.
# 🎯 Objectives
• To track total sales, footfall, and average spending per person.

• To analyze sales patterns by hour, day, and month.

• To evaluate the performance of store locations based on revenue and footfall.

• To identify top-performing products and understand category distribution.

• To assess order sizes and sales performance across different product types and sizes.

• To provide store managers with clear visual insights for decision-making.
# ❓ Business Questions Addressed
1. How do sales vary by day of the week and hour of the day?

2. Are there any peak times for sales activity?

3. What is the total sales revenue by month?

4. How do sales differ across store locations?

5. What is the average bill and order per person?

6. Which products are the best-sellers by revenue?

7. How is sales performance distributed by product category?

8. What is the order size distribution (small, regular, large)?

9. How do weekend vs. weekday orders compare?
# 📌 Findings and Conclusion
• Total Sales: $698,812.33

• Total Footfall: 149,116 customers

• Average Bill per Person: $4.69

• Average Order per Person: 1.44

• Sales peak between 8 AM to 11 AM, aligning with morning coffee demand.

• Barista Espresso is the top-selling product, generating over $91,000 in revenue.

• Coffee (39%) and Branded Products (28%) are the most dominant categories by sales.

• Large-sized orders constitute the highest share (34%) of all orders.

• Among locations, Hell’s Kitchen has the highest revenue at $236,511.17.

• Footfall is consistent across weekdays, with a noticeable dip on Saturday.
# Conclusion:
The dashboard effectively reveals how time, product preference, and store location influence sales in a coffee shop environment. These insights help streamline operational planning, inventory management, and promotional strategies for peak periods.
# 📈 Strategic Insights Summary
This Excel dashboard delivers valuable insights into the coffee shop's sales dynamics, helping stakeholders understand customer habits and product performance. It empowers managers to target peak hours, stock best-selling items, and refine service across high-traffic locations—ultimately driving smarter decisions to boost profitability and customer satisfaction.
